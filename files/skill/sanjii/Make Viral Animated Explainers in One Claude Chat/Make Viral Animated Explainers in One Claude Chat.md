Four Styles You Can Ask For



3D explainer

What it does: Writes a viral script on your topic and turns it into a finished vertical short in the style you name. This is step three from the video.

Using the animation skill, write a viral script about [your topic] and create the video in [your style].

The style can be anything you can describe. These four are the ones from the video:

3D explainer. Glossy, stylized 3D that shows how something works from the inside.
Stickman animation. Simple stick figures, fast action and a lot of humor.
Paper collage. Cutouts, headlines and maps, like a news explainer.
Low-poly "what if". A blocky video-game look for "what would happen if" stories.
Prompt 2 — Let Claude Pick the Idea
