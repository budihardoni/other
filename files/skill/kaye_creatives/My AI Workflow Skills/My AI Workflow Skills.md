## Skill 4. Editing Talking Head Reels
```
talking-head-reels.zip
```

This is the skill I use to edit my talking head videos. You just drop in your raw video, and Claude does the whole edit for you with pro-level motion graphics.

What it does
It cuts out your pauses and filler words, cleans up your voice, and adds bold moving text, split screens, smooth transitions and film grain. Every few seconds something changes on screen based on what you’re saying, so the video never feels boring. You get back a finished vertical reel ready for Instagram, TikTok or YouTube Shorts.

How I use it 🎥
I record myself talking, drop the video in, and Claude plans the edit first so I can approve it before it renders. If the Higgsfield MCP is connected, it can also generate AI B-roll that matches exactly what I’m talking about.

How to use it
Add the skill, upload your raw video, tell Claude your call to action at the end, and let it do the rest ✨
